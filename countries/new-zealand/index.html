WHY I WENT

The story of
New Zealand.

A campervan and the South Island turned out to be a near-perfect combination — every day ended somewhere different, usually with a mountain in view.

The hiking did most of the work here: long alpine trails, glacier valleys and lakes so clear they barely looked real.

Wildlife kept turning up where I least expected it — kea squabbling on alpine rocks, a falcon on its nest, penguin crossing signs along the coast — and the autumn light on Aoraki / Mount Cook, Lake Wānaka and Lake Wakatipu made every stop feel like a postcard.
