WHY I WENT

The story of
Vietnam.

The Ha Giang Loop was the centrepiece of this trip — days on a motorbike through northern mountains that kept getting more dramatic around every bend.

From caves in the north to the pace of the south, Vietnam turned into one of the best road trips on this list.

Off the bike there was just as much variety: the limestone bays around Ha Long, boat rides between the forested karsts of Ninh Binh, and sunset over the coast at Da Nang.
