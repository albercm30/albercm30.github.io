WHY I WENT

The story of
Japan.

Japan is a country built for people who like details to be right — the trains, the food, the way a whole city can be quiet and considerate at once.

Most of the trip revolved around eating: proper ramen, wagyu that justified the hype, and enough convenience-store snacks to embarrass myself slightly.
