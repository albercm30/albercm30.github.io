DATA LAYER

Every hike becomes data.

Distance, elevation gain and duration are pulled straight from Strava. The elevation profiles are built from the underlying GPX tracks, cleaned to remove GPS glitches (a few of these files had physically impossible elevation spikes — likely satellite lock issues — filtered out before charting). Acatenango is still waiting on a GPX file.
