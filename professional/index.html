Bachelor's Degree at UC3M

Bachelor Thesis — Measuring Energy Consumption of Online Ads on Social Networks

  • Developed an automated Python/Selenium data-collection pipeline to identify, scrape and embed Facebook and Twitter ads and posts, collecting 7,277 items for analysis.
  • Built the methodology to extract HTML content and associated metadata, then measured loading time, internal network calls and estimated CPU/GPU/RAM energy consumption for each item.
  • Analysed the resulting datasets to identify factors affecting energy consumption, finding that Facebook ads consumed 1.6× more energy than posts and Facebook ads consumed approximately 8.7× more energy than Twitter ads.
