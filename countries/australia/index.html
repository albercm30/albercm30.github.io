WHY I WENT

Wild at
every scale.

Australia was one of those trips where the reason for going was simple: wildlife.

I wanted to see what the country looked like beyond the cities — rainforest, reef, beaches, remote roads and the animals that make Australia unlike anywhere else.

The trip became a mix of diving on the Great Barrier Reef, exploring the Daintree, searching for cassowaries and platypuses, driving between places that looked ridiculously far apart on a map, and stopping whenever something interesting appeared.
