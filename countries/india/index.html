WHY I WENT

The story of
India.

India doesn't ease you in. Between the colour, the noise, the food and the sheer number of people, it's the kind of place that reorders how you think a city can work.

Varanasi was the centre of gravity for this trip — watching life, ritual and the Ganges move together on the ghats at dawn is not something that leaves you.
