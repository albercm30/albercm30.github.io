WHY I WENT

The story of
Australia.

Australia was one of those trips where the reason for going was simple: wildlife.

I wanted to see what the country looked like beyond the cities — rainforest, reef, beaches, remote roads and the animals that make Australia unlike anywhere else. The trip became a mix of diving on the Great Barrier Reef, exploring the Daintree, searching for cassowaries and platypuses, driving between places that looked ridiculously far apart on a map, and stopping whenever something interesting appeared.

Away from the far north, the Queensland coast added a gentler side: koalas dozing in eucalyptus trees, kangaroos and kookaburras in the bush, a waterfall in the Gold Coast hinterland, and beach days — and plenty of jump photos — with friends.

The trip also took in Sydney — the harbour, the Opera House and the skyline from the water — and a humpback whale surfacing offshore.
