WHY I WENT

The story of
Bulgaria.

Bulgaria surprised me more than almost anywhere else in Europe — a lively capital, Plovdiv's old town and the Rila mountains, all with barely any crowds.

It's the kind of country that quietly outperforms its reputation once you actually spend time there.
