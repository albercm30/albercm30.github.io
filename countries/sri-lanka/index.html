WHY I WENT

The story of
Sri Lanka.

The train from Kandy to Ella is one of those routes people talk about for good reason — hours of tea plantations and mountains rolling past an open door. The day I rode it the whole hill country was wrapped in fog.

Sri Lanka packed a surprising range into a small island: ancient stupas and the rock fortress of Sigiriya, jungle rivers, elephants and monkeys, and a coast of palm-lined railway lines and surf bays.

I climbed neighbouring Pidurangala at sunset to see Sigiriya from above, walked around the white stupas of Anuradhapura, floated down a rainforest river in lifejackets and helmets with friends, and watched an elephant bathe in a lake. Toque macaques and a crested serpent eagle turned up along the way, and there were beach sunsets and a stretch of south-coast railway between the palms.
