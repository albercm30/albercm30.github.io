WHY I WENT

The story of
Mexico.

Mexico turned into one of the best diving trips on this list — Baja California's marine life is hard to overstate.

From sea lions and mobula rays in the Pacific to the cenotes and reefs of the Yucatán, the country delivered on both coasts.

On land it was just as rewarding: the colonial streets and Mayan ruins of the Yucatán, flamingos wading in the shallows, and birds like the turquoise-browed motmot.
